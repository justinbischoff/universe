# The Making of Earth

An interactive, animated timeline of the universe told from Earth's point of view: where its elements were forged, how the Sun and planet formed, the first cells, the first trees, and how Earth's story ends.

**Live page:** https://justinbischoff.github.io/universe/

- `index.html` is the whole app: a single page with no build step and no dependencies apart from Google Fonts.
- `timeline.md` lists all 32 events with dates, explanations, and notes on disputed estimates.

## Controls

- **Play/pause:** button or spacebar
- **Step through events:** arrow buttons or ← / →
- **Jump:** drag along the timeline, or click the to-scale bar
